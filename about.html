About Shalom Love Home Care

Where peace and love meet everyday care.

Our Story

Care That Feels Like Family

"Shalom" is a word of peace, wholeness, and wellbeing. It is the feeling we want every family to have when they welcome us into their home.

Shalom Love Home Care was built on a simple belief: the people who raised us, taught us, and cared for us deserve to be cared for with the same love, in the place they call home.

We know how hard it is to see someone you love need more help than before. That is why we do more than complete tasks. We listen, we learn each person's routines and preferences, and we match them with a caregiver who feels less like a stranger and more like a trusted friend of the family.
